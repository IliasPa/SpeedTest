Download
How fast data reaches you. It decides what video quality you can stream and how quickly pages and files load — the number most people mean by "internet speed".
Upload
How fast data leaves your device: video calls, sending files to cloud storage, live streaming. Home connections are usually far slower upward than downward, and that is normal.
Ping
How long one message takes to reach the server and come back, in milliseconds. Low ping is what makes a connection feel instant. Gaming and calls care about this far more than about speed.
Jitter
How much the ping varies from one message to the next. Steady is good — high jitter is what makes a call stutter or a game jump, even when the ping and speed both look fine.
