When the line is busy

Speed is measured on an idle line, but you rarely use an idle line. These are taken while the test is saturating your connection, which is when calls stutter and games lag. A fast connection can still fail here — and that failure is what people actually notice.

    What you can do

    Each row checks your measured speed and ping against what that activity actually needs. A tick means your connection clears the bar with room to spare; a cross names the one thing that falls short.

      How long things take

      File size divided by the speed just measured. Treat these as a best case: the far end, your disk and your Wi-Fi each add limits of their own, so real transfers usually take a little longer.

        At the same time

        How many of each your line could carry at once, everyone sharing it together — one person on 4K in the living room while another is on a call upstairs.
